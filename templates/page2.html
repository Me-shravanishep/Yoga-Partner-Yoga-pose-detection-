Tree Pose (Vrikshasana)

BENEFITS

Improves concentration and balance. Strengthens leg muscles.

CARE & PRECAUTION

Butterfly Pose (Baddha Konasana)

BENEFITS

Opens the hips and stretches inner thighs. Calms the mind and reduces anxiety.

CARE & PRECAUTION

Don't force the knees down to the floor. Let gravity do the work.

Cobra Pose (Bhujangasana)

BENEFITS

Strengthens the spine and opens the chest. Relieves stress and fatigue.

CARE & PRECAUTION

Don't crunch the lower back. Only lift as high as comfortable without straining.

Adho Mukha Svanasana

BENEFITS

Strengthens upper body and arms. Increases blood flow to the brain for better focus.

CARE & PRECAUTION

Spread fingers wide to distribute weight and protect wrists.

Child's Pose (Balasana)

BENEFITS

Gently stretches the lower back and hips. Calms the nervous system and regulates emotions.

CARE & PRECAUTION

Ensure chest isn't compressed so it's easy to breathe deeply.

Cat-Cow Pose (Marjaryasana-Bitilasana)

BENEFITS

Gently warms up the spine and increases flexibility. Helps learn to engage abdominal muscles.

CARE & PRECAUTION

When looking up, don't crunch the back of the neck. Keep the gaze slightly forward.
